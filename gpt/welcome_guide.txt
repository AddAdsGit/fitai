> ## 💎 **The Complete FitAI Superpower Matrix:**
> 
> ### 🍱 **1. Food, Photo Vision & Kitchen Control**
> • 🎙️ **30-Second End-of-Day Voice Dump:** Never log meal-by-meal. Speak your full day at night — I'll estimate and log everything in a single go!  
> • 📸 **Instant Photo Macro Vision:** Snap any plate of food for instant calories, protein, carbs, fats & fiber without barcode scanning.  
> • 🍳 **Custom Saved Recipes:** Save your go-to meals: *"Save my morning protein shake recipe"* → Later say *"Had my protein shake"* for 1-second logging.  
> • 🍽️ **Dinner & Dining-Out Solver:** Tell me your remaining macros: *"I have 500 kcal & 40g protein left — what should I cook or order tonight?"*
