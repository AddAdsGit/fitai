# FITAI MASTER CAPABILITIES, FEATURES & BENEFITS DIRECTORY
===================================================================

This document is the complete authoritative encyclopedia of all features, capabilities, benefits, and persona use cases available in FitAI. 

When a user asks "What can you do?", "How can you help me?", or asks about features, consult the user's active profile (goals, tracked nutrients, knowledge) and dynamically personalize the most relevant superpowers to their exact lifestyle. If their profile is new or empty, showcase the breadth of these features.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
1. CORE PILLARS & AUDIENCE PERSONAS
-------------------------------------------------------------------
A. THE MUSCLE BUILDER & ATHLETE (Hypertrophy & Strength)
   - Goal: Hit daily protein targets, fuel workouts, optimize recovery.
   - FitAI Superpowers: Instant protein estimation on plates, 1-shot custom protein shake recipes, 30-day protein consistency charts, pre/post workout meal tagging, high-protein meal prep grocery lists.

B. THE FAT LOSS & BODY COMPOSITION OPTIMIZER (Lean & Consistent)
   - Goal: Maintain a sustainable caloric deficit without food anxiety or tedious math.
   - FitAI Superpowers: Active 30-day average weight tracking (no scale panic), dinner dilemma solver (what to eat with remaining calories), restaurant dining-out guides, photo macro estimates without weighing food.

C. THE GUT HEALTH & WELLNESS INVESTIGATOR (IBS, Bloating & Digestion)
   - Goal: Eliminate bloating, optimize microbiome transit, discover inflammatory food triggers.
   - FitAI Superpowers: Bloating severity tracker (Levels 1-4), Bristol Stool scale logging (Types 1-7), 14-day food trigger correlation engine ("What caused my bloating this week?"), fiber intake optimization.

D. THE BUSY PROFESSIONAL & HIGH PERFORMER (Zero Friction & Vitality)
   - Goal: Eliminate afternoon brain fog, eat consciously with zero time wasted.
   - FitAI Superpowers: 30-Second End-of-Day Voice Dump (speak all meals once at night), vitality & energy crash tracking (Levels 1-5), automated Telegram reminders, zero-math incremental water logging.

-------------------------------------------------------------------
2. COMPLETE FEATURE CATALOG & REAL-WORLD COMMANDS
-------------------------------------------------------------------

1. PHOTO NUTRITION VISION
   - What It Does: Analyzes photos of plates, bowls, snacks, or drinks via vision AI. Estimates portion sizes, calories, and all enabled custom nutrients (protein, carbs, fats, fiber, sodium, caffeine, etc.).
   - Commands: Upload any photo from camera roll or take a live picture.

2. 30-SECOND END-OF-DAY VOICE DUMP (Natural Language Logging)
   - What It Does: Users don't need to log after every bite. Speak all meals, drinks, and snacks in one natural paragraph or voice recording. FitAI splits them into separate meals and logs them accurately.
   - Command Example: "For breakfast had 2 eggs and coffee, lunch was a chicken rice bowl, afternoon had a protein bar and 1L water, energy was 4/5."

3. DYNAMIC CUSTOM NUTRIENTS (Beyond Basic Macros)
   - What It Does: FitAI dynamically tracks whatever the user configured — Protein, Carbs, Fats, Fiber, Sodium, Caffeine, Iron, Potassium, Saturated Fat, Cholesterol, Sugars, etc.
   - Conversational Reconfiguration: Users can change tracking metrics directly in chat.
   - Command Example: "Start tracking caffeine for me" or "Change my daily protein goal to 160g".

4. DINNER & DINING-OUT PROBLEM SOLVER
   - What It Does: Calculates remaining daily calories and macros, and recommends tailored recipes from fridge ingredients or exact orders at restaurants.
   - Command Example: "I have 550 kcal and 42g protein left today — what should I cook with chicken and eggs, or order if I eat out?"

5. SMART GROCERY LISTS & MEAL PREP
   - What It Does: Generates structured, aisle-by-aisle shopping lists designed to hit the user's weekly calorie and macro budget.
   - Command Example: "Build a 5-day high-protein grocery list for my weekly meal prep under 2,000 kcal/day."

6. SAVED RECIPES & KITCHEN SYSTEM
   - What It Does: Saves custom complex multi-ingredient home recipes under named shortcuts for 1-second future logging.
   - Command Example: "Save my morning protein shake recipe (whey, almond milk, peanut butter, banana)" -> Later: "Had my protein shake".

7. GUT COMFORT & BLOATING DETECTIVE
   - What It Does: Logs bloating on a 1-4 scale and correlates meals over 7-90 days to identify trigger foods (dairy, high sodium, excess FODMAPs).
   - Command Example: "Mild bloating after lunch" -> Later: "What foods caused my bloating over the last 2 weeks?"

8. DIGESTION & BRISTOL STOOL SCALE TRACKING
   - What It Does: Logs stool quality (Bristol scale 1-7) with size and timing to monitor gut transit and microbiome health.
   - Command Example: "Had a smooth type 4 bowel movement at 9 AM."

9. VITALITY & ENERGY CRASH ANALYTICS
   - What It Does: Rates daily energy (1-5) and identifies which lunches or high-glycemic foods cause 3 PM afternoon slumps.
   - Command Example: "Energy is 2/5 today, feeling sluggish after pasta."

10. ZERO-MATH SMART HYDRATION
    - What It Does: Seamlessly adds fluids without requiring the user to calculate totals.
    - Command Example: "Drank 500ml of water" -> Later: "Had another glass" (auto-adds +250ml).

11. TRUE ACTIVE WEIGHT TRACKING
    - What It Does: Calculates true statistical averages across active logged days, preventing anxiety from daily water-weight fluctuations.
    - Command Example: "Weighed 70.4kg this morning."

12. LIVING AUTONOMOUS HEALTH MEMORY
    - What It Does: Permanently stores allergies, dislikes, dietary restrictions, workout routines, and preferences across every chat.
    - Command Example: "Remember I avoid dairy, dislike cilantro, and lift weights at 6 PM on weekdays."

13. TIMEZONE AUTO-SYNC
    - What It Does: Automatically updates the user's profile timezone when traveling so all logs reflect local time.
    - Command Example: "I'm traveling in London right now" or "Set timezone to Europe/London".

14. TELEGRAM REMINDERS & AUTOMATED REPORTS
    - What It Does: Configures automated meal reminders and evening recap reports sent straight to Telegram.
    - Command Example: "Set my Telegram reminders to 9 AM and 8 PM."

15. 1-TAP AESTHETIC WEB SHARECARDS
    - What It Does: Every meal logged generates a sleek, visual web link to share progress with friends or coaches.

16. DEEP INTERACTIVE TREND CHARTS
    - What It Does: Generates visual charts (bar, line, trend) for 7, 30, or 90 days of calories, macros, weight, or bloat patterns.
    - Command Example: "Chart my 30-day weight trend and calorie consistency."

17. FULL EDIT & DELETE CONTROL
    - What It Does: Update or remove any past log via conversation.
    - Command Example: "Change my lunch carbs to 50g" or "Delete the snack I logged at 3 PM."

18. HANDS-FREE VOICE CONVERSATION MODE (Continuous Voice Logging)
    - What It Does: Speak naturally in ChatGPT Voice Mode while walking, driving, or cooking. Whatever you say (meals, water, weight, vitals, allergies), FitAI immediately executes background database calls and replies with a short 1-sentence audio confirmation so you never have to read your screen.
    - Command Example: "Hey, I just had a chicken bowl and 500ml water, and I weighed 70kg this morning." -> FitAI logs all three instantly and confirms over audio!
